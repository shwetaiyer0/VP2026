ABOUT THE EVENT

Rediscover
Right Living

A human being is designed for clarity, not confusion; for peace, not agitation. Yet, over time we began to believe that stress and strain are routine, that doubt and disappointment are inevitable. The world teaches you to look outside for answers — to achieve more, acquire more, aspire more. Vedanta teaches you to look within and discover that you have always been complete, whole, fulfilled.

Come rediscover the technique of right living. Explore the knowledge that reveals your essential nature, exposes what happiness truly is, and awakens a well-being rooted in wisdom. Heed this Call to Happiness!

AN ANCIENT MANUAL FOR MODERN LIFE

Why Gita?

The Bhagavad Gita is a timeless guide for everyday living. Through its profound yet practical teachings, you cultivate clearer thinking, stronger values, and a calm mind; thus, your actions become more effective and your life more harmonious and happy.

Study the Gita and understand the forces that drive stress, anxiety, and fear—and learn how to act with intention rather than impulse, to cultivate calm even in chaos, and to unveil the source of internal strength that no external circumstance can shake.

The key to happiness is with you.

4 EVENINGS

Clearer thought,
purer feeling,
calmer being

The 14th chapter of the Bhagavad Gita is entitled ‘The Yoga of the Three Gunas’. It reveals the three fundamental qualities of nature (gunas) that shape your actions, emotions and thoughts. It provides an understanding of how these gunas function, which helps one rise above the limitations they impose on the personality.

In these four evenings you will learn how to recognize your deeper tendencies, reduce agitation and inertia, and establish calm and clarity within. Thereafter, your life is one of greater balance, greater resilience and greater happiness.

Regardless of your present spiritual status
you must direct your efforts toward your evolution.


The direction of your energies makes or mars you.

- A. PARTHASARATHY,
BHAGAVAD GITA CH.14

ABOUT THE SPEAKER

Sunandaji

Sunanda Leelaram, affectionately known as Sunandaji, is the daughter-disciple of Swami A. Parthasarathy. Under his guidance, Sunandaji has been imbibing Vedanta, the ancient philosophy of India for over four decades.

Sunandaji has addressed diverse audiences across four continents for the past forty years including Young Presidents’ Organisation, Harvard Business School, EY. Her clear and powerful presentation of ancient wisdom in contemporary thought has received widespread coverage in television, radio and print media.

TRADITION

Gita Yajna

For over five decades, Swami A. Parthasarathy conducted the Gita Jnana Yajna, a public lecture series on the Bhagavad Gita, across India and the globe. The effort was to make the invaluable wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita accessible to all humanity.

The tradition continues through Sunandaji, daughter-disciple of Swamiji.
