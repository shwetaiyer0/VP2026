Step back. Take stock

Step back. Take stock

A chance to pause, reflect and return to wisdom that has guided human beings for generations.

Only human beings require guidance in living because they possess freedom of choice through the intellect. Animals live according to instinct and therefore maintain perfect discipline without instruction.

Without fundamental values, confusion and stress arise in life and society. Conversely, a life based on such values becomes dynamic, purposeful and satisfying.

This online lecture invites you to reflect on the principles that form the foundation of a well-lived life.

BUILD A MEANINGFUL LIFE

Direction


Busyness is not the same as purpose. People remain constantly busy but seldom question the purpose of their work. Without a clear ideal, effort becomes scattered, results remain limited and one never feels content. A higher purpose brings direction and fulfillment in action.

Discipline and Objectivity


Once direction is established, many temptations arise that attempt to divert one’s attention. Discipline protects one’s purpose and sustains progress. When emotions and impulses dominate, clarity of judgment is lost. Leading to poor decision-making. Success in any profession or field requires the capacity to remain objective and composed.

Gratitude


No life is lived alone. Every human being depends on the support of others - society, nature, family, teachers and countless unseen contributors. Gratitude is not merely a good feeling; it is a value that brings humility and peace into daily living.

Swami A. Parthasarathy

Swami A. Parthasarathy

Renowned philosopher, author and internationally respected authority on Vedanta, the ancient Indian philosophy of life and living. For over 70 years, Swami A. Parthasarathy has dedicated himself to the study, research and dissemination of Vedanta. He has authored twelve books and founded the Vedanta Academy in Malavli, India - an institution devoted to the systematic study of this timeless knowledge.

Affectionately addressed as Swamiji, he has addressed audiences across the world, including leaders, professionals, students and seekers from diverse backgrounds. His work has also been widely acclaimed and featured in international press and television media.

Through his clear, practical and rational presentation of Vedanta, Swamiji has helped generations understand how ancient wisdom can be applied to modern life including work, relationships and inner growth.

Tradition that Inspires

Tradition that Inspires

Guru Purnima is an ancient Indian tradition that honours the galaxy of sages and saints who have contributed towards and passed on higher values of life for the benefit of humanity for thousands of years. It also provides us an opportunity to pause and reflect on the timeless wisdom that they expounded.
