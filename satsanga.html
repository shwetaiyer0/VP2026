Vedanta Satsanga

SUNDAY, 25 OCTOBER | 9:30 AM TO 11:30 AM

Pingale Sanai Hall, Koregaon Park

VIEW IN GOOGLE MAPS

About the Event

Satsanga brings together the members of Vedanta Pune’s study classes, a growing community of seekers united by the commitment to self-development and spiritual unfoldment. Drawn from all walks of life—students, professionals, businesspersons, homemakers, and retirees—they come together in the shared pursuit of inner growth through the timeless wisdom of Vedanta.
Over the years, Satsanga has become an annual feature in the Vedanta Pune calendar. An occasion for members from study groups across Pune to deepen their engagement with Vedanta and interact meaningfully with one another. The day is marked by a talk on Vedanta and opportunities for dialogue and exchange among fellow seekers.

Held just before the Gita Jnana Yajna in Pune, Satsanga also serves as a platform for members to gather in the spirit of service, discuss and plan for the upcoming event, and volunteer for the many roles required to conduct the yajna successfully.
More than an annual gathering, Satsanga is a reminder to reaffirm one’s commitment to the path of self-development and Self-realisation. It is an opportunity to connect with like-minded seekers, draw inspiration from each other and work together as a community to share this life-transforming knowledge with the people of Pune.

Program:

9:30 am 'The Adhikari', talk by Dr. Jal
10:45 am Announcements followed by Interaction
