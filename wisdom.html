Bhaja Govindam
Seek the Self

Bhaja Govindam, composed by Adi Shankaracharya, is a timeless guide to living wisely in a world of constant change. It reminds us to shift our focus from fleeting pursuits to what truly brings meaning and inner fulfillment. Through simple yet powerful verses, it offers practical insights for a peaceful, productive and purposeful life.

About the Speaker

SARISHA BHARUCHA

Sarisha Bharucha is a senior disciple of the world-renowned philosopher Swami A. Parthasarathy. After graduating from the three-year residential course at the Vedanta Academy, Sarisha has spent over two decades researching and propagating the timeless wisdom of Vedanta. Her clear presentation of the subtle truths, and use of contemporary examples, have benefitted audiences across all ages and backgrounds.
